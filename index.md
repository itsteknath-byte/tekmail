# Tek Mail Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

Tek Mail is an ad-free Android email client for Gmail and Google Workspace accounts.

## What Tek Mail accesses
With your permission, Tek Mail uses the Gmail API scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify` to read, label, archive, trash, draft and send your email. It cannot permanently delete mail. It reads your account's email address to label the account in the app.

## Where your data goes
- **Your device and Google only.** Tek Mail talks directly to Google's Gmail API. It has no server of its own. Your email never passes through, and is never stored on, any server run by the developer.
- **Local storage.** Messages, drafts, and attachments you open are cached in the app's private storage on your device so you can read them offline. Android backup and device transfer of this data are disabled.
- **Sign-in.** Tek Mail never sees your Google password. Google Play services handles sign-in and gives the app short-lived access tokens, which are not saved or logged.
- **No ads, analytics, tracking, or AI processing.** No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Notifications
New-mail notifications show the sender and subject. On a locked screen they show only that new mail arrived.

## Removing your data
- **Settings → Remove account** revokes Tek Mail's access at Google and deletes that account's local data.
- **Uninstalling** the app deletes all local data.
- **Revoke access at any time** at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Tek Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
